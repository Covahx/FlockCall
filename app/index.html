Please read first

If you have found a wild duckling, contact a licensed wildlife rehabilitator before anything else. Trained rehabbers and species-appropriate housing give the bird its best chance.

FlockCall is a stopgap. It plays a small set of natural merganser calls to reduce stress during short-term care, never to bond the duckling with a human. Keep your voice low, your movements slow, and your face out of view as much as possible.

How to use it well

Use the app sparingly. Long silence is good. Outdoor time near water, with no playback, is better than any button in this app.

Keep the speaker at arm's length from the bird. Never point it directly at the duckling from close range. If the bird startles or freezes, stop and give it quiet space.

When the duckling stabilizes, turn on Taper Mode in settings. The app will become harder to use each day, by design.

What the three buttons mean

Heart: calm / reassurance call when the duckling needs settling.

Fish and arrows: follow and feeding context — use sparingly during moves or near meal time.

Exclamation: sharp alert, very rare. Tap twice within a few seconds to confirm before it plays.

Built-in limits

The app enforces cooldowns between plays, a daily soft cap of 8 plays, a hard cap of 20, and a long-press requirement during quiet hours (22:00 to 06:00). These are not suggestions.

About

FlockCall is an offline, ad-free, telemetry-free prototype. Source audio ethically (Xeno-canto, Macaulay Library) before any use with a real bird.
